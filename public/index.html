How it works

One dashboard, from camera roll to posted.

  1. 1

    Pick the business

    Restaurant, detailer, tradie, barber, gym, or describe your own. Editing and copy adapt to it.

  2. 2

    Drop in raw footage

    Upload the clips straight off your phone. Long, shaky or messy is fine.

  3. 3

    Generate clips

    Best moments are found, dead time is cut, and a hook, captions and a CTA are burned into vertical video.

  4. 4

    Post with copy that fits

    Captions, hashtags, hooks and a 7-day plan, ready to copy. Download the MP4s and go.

AI video editing

Claude reviews frames from your footage alongside measured motion, audio and lighting, then writes an exact edit list. FFmpeg renders it.

  • Finds the money shots: sizzle, foam, the finished job
  • Cuts setup, walking around and dark or shaky bits
  • Opening hook, captions and CTA in platform-safe zones
  • Smart 9:16 crop, or a blurred fill for landscape footage

AI marketing

Tell it the business name, suburb, services and tone. Get copy that sounds like the business, not a template.

  • Captions, hooks and calls to action
  • Broad, niche and local hashtags
  • Video concepts with shot lists
  • 7-day content plan and promo posts
